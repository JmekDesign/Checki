# Checki — Support Knowledge Base

## What is Checki?
Checki is a web app for bars, cafes, bakeries and restaurants. Staff opens tabs (checks), adds items, closes them with payment. Managers see stats, archive, cash register, catalog.

## How to log in
Go to admin.checki.ge. Enter your login and password provided by the manager. If you forgot your password, tap "Forgot password" on the login screen.

## How to open a new check
On the main screen tap the green "+" button (bottom right). Enter guest name or leave empty. Tap "Open check".

## How to add items to a check
Open a check → tap the input field at the bottom → type item name and price (e.g. "Beer 8") or choose from the suggestion list. Tap "Add". You can also use voice input — tap the microphone button.

## How to use voice input
Open a check → tap the microphone button → speak the items (e.g. "Two beers and a coffee"). The app will parse and add the items automatically.

## How to close a check (take payment)
Open a check → tap "Close check" → choose payment method (cash / card / split) → confirm.

## How to send a digital receipt to the guest (QR code)
When closing a check, a QR code is shown in the payment confirmation screen. The guest can scan it with their phone camera to open a digital receipt at checki.ge/r/ — no app needed. This is a paperless receipt sent directly to the guest's phone.

## How to delete a check
Open a check → tap the trash icon → confirm deletion. Only managers can delete closed checks from the archive.

## How to find a closed check
Tap "Archive" (bottom tab) → use date filters or search by guest name.

## How to add staff (managers only)
Go to Venue Settings (gear icon) → Staff section → tap "+ Add" → fill name, login, password → Save.

## How to change interface language
Go to Venue Settings → scroll down → tap EN or ქა button.

## How to use the product catalog
Venue Settings → Product catalog. Add products with name, price, category. Staff can then quickly pick items from the suggestion list when adding to a check.

## How to scan a paper receipt
On the main screen tap the scan icon → take a photo of the receipt → the app will parse items automatically. Review and confirm.

## How to scan a menu to fill the catalog
Venue Settings → Product catalog → Scan menu. Take photos of menu pages → AI will extract items and prices → review and add to catalog.

## How to use the cash register
Venue Settings → Cash register. Record cash movements (deposits/withdrawals). See balance by date range. Export PDF report.

## How to use supplies / procurement
Venue Settings → Supplies. Record purchased supplies with cost. Track spending over time.

## Subscription and pricing
Checki costs 49 GEL/month or 490 GEL/year. New venues get a 14-day free trial. To pay: go to Venue Settings → Subscription → choose plan → follow TBC payment instructions. After payment write to @CheckiService_Bot.

## I forgot my password
On the login screen tap "Forgot password" → enter your email → check your email for reset link.

## The app is not working / I see an error
Try refreshing the page. If the problem persists, describe what happened and we will help.

## I cannot add items (blocked)
Your venue subscription may have expired. The manager should go to Venue Settings → Subscription to renew.
