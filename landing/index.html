FAQ

Frequently asked questions

?

Do I need a cash register to use Checki?

No. Checki runs in any smartphone browser — no hardware, no cash register, no printer required. Just a phone with internet.

?

How is Checki different from iiko or Square?

iiko and Square are built for large restaurants with expensive hardware and long setup. Checki is for bars and small cafes: ready in 5 minutes, runs on your personal phone, zero installation.

?

Can I manage multiple venues?

Yes. Checki supports multiple venues under one account. Each venue's data is fully isolated — staff from one bar cannot see another's orders.

?

How do I track bar tabs without a POS system?

A bartender opens a tab, adds items from the smart catalog, and closes with payment method. The manager sees live revenue and stats. All in a phone browser, nothing to install.
